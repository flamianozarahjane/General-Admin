How I Work

Six habits that keep your records trustworthy

✓

Verify against the source

Every entry traces back to a document, email, or system record. If I can't source it, I flag it instead of guessing.

⌗

Consistent naming and structure

Files and folders follow one convention so anyone on the team can find a record without asking me where it is.

Clear written summaries

Research comes back as findings and a recommendation — not a pile of links for you to sort through.

Confidentiality by default
